# Advisor Demo and Outreach Guide

## Purpose

This document captures practical guidance for advisor-led outreach while InsurFlow is in client-first MVP mode.

It is intentionally concise and focused on what helps advisors convert demo interest into next conversations.

---

## Core Outreach Reality

- Many first interactions are cold outreach.
- The demo is often the first product impression.
- Advisors need to show value quickly, without setup overhead.

Product implication:

- The demo flow is not a side feature. It is a primary acquisition and validation channel.

---

## Demo Outcomes We Need

Every demo should make these points clear:

- The platform can gather key client context quickly.
- The output is easy to understand and explain.
- The advisor can move from demo to a real follow-up action.

---

## Messaging Guidelines for Advisors

Use this framing in demos and outbound:

- "This helps us quickly estimate your current protection gap."
- "You get a clear snapshot first, then we go deeper together."
- "The goal is clarity and next steps, not a complicated report."

Avoid promising long-tail features not in MVP.

---

## Product UX Priorities from Outreach

1. Fast entry with clear time expectation.
2. Guided questions in plain language.
3. Snapshot result that can be discussed live.
4. Advisor handoff CTA that is obvious.

If a proposed feature does not improve one of these, it likely belongs in Post-MVP.

---

## MVP vs Post-MVP Framing

- Use **MVP** and **Post-MVP** labels in all planning docs.
- Do not use old tier sequencing terminology.
- Keep issue labels simple so any team member can pick up work quickly.

---

## Feedback Capture During Demos

Advisors should capture:

- Where users hesitate in the flow.
- Terms users do not understand.
- Where users ask "what do I do next?"

This feedback should directly drive iteration on `src/app/demo/*` before broad scope expansion.
